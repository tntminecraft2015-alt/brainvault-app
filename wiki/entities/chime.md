# Chime

A US financial technology company that offers mobile-first banking. Chime is **not a bank**: its banking services and the Chime Card are provided by partner banks.

## Chime Prime (as of 2026-10)

A membership tier unlocked by **qualifying direct deposits of $3,000+ per month**. It is not based on balance.
- 5% cash back on one spending category the member picks each month (Chime Card; terms and limits apply)
- 4.00% APY on savings, no minimum balance
- Travel perks such as airport lounge access (additional terms)
- No mandatory subscription fee

This is the main example of a [[rewards-checking-account]] in the wiki.
