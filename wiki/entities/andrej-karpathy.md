# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher, educator, and former Director of AI at Tesla and founding member of OpenAI. Known for accessible writing and teaching on deep learning and LLMs.

## Background

Karpathy did his PhD at Stanford under Fei-Fei Li, working on deep learning for visual recognition. He joined OpenAI as a founding member in 2015, then led the Autopilot vision team at Tesla for several years before returning to OpenAI. He is widely known for his educational content — the *Neural Networks: Zero to Hero* series and *makemore* / *nanoGPT* projects — which have made deep learning accessible to a broad audience.
