# Rewards Checking Account

A checking account that pays cash back or a higher savings rate for everyday use, usually gated by direct deposit rather than by a minimum balance, and usually with no annual fee.

## Overview

Traditionally, banks tied premium perks (better interest rates, premium tiers, travel benefits) to how much money a customer kept on deposit. The rewards checking model uses **behavior** instead, most often a monthly payroll direct deposit threshold. In practice, someone with a steady paycheck but little savings can qualify for perks that used to require thousands of dollars sitting idle.

So far the only source on this concept is sponsored content from [[chime]] ([[chime-bank-rewards-everyday-habits]]). That source sets up the product category in a way that favors Chime's own offering, so treat the framing with caution until an independent source covers it.

## Key Properties

- **Gate:** monthly direct deposit amount, not account balance (example: Chime Prime requires $3,000+/month)
- **Rewards:** category cash back on a debit card, an elevated savings APY, and sometimes travel perks
- **Cost:** no annual or subscription fee, which is pitched against annual-fee rewards credit cards

## Relationships

- **Contrasts with:** balance-tiered premium checking and annual-fee rewards credit cards
- **Example:** [[chime]] (Chime Prime tier)
