- **Newport Music Hall** (Oak View Group) — part-time bartender, same pay/benefit structure as KEMBA Live, evergreen posting. Apply: https://www.teamworkonline.com/arenas-facilities-jobs/oakview-group-/ovg-corporate/bartender-part-time-newport-music-hall-2149761
- **A&R Music Bar / LC Pavilion / The Basement** (PromoWest Productions, owned by AEG Presents — three of Columbus's core indie/touring venues) — guest services and bar/production staff roles. Apply: https://aegworldwide.com/careers/jobs/AEGLV3840/guest-services-staff---promowest---columbus (check AEG Worldwide careers site for current bartender-specific postings under "PromoWest")
