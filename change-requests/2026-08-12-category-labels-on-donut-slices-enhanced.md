## Implementation notes

Place category names directly on the outer edge of each donut slice in high-contrast colored bands with white text, positioned tangent to the slice perimeter. This eliminates the separate legend entirely—users read spending instantly at a glance without cross-referencing. The enhanced label bands use dark backgrounds and bold typography to stand out sharply from the pie chart, making them scannable even on mobile.

Source: [Budget Tracker App UI/UX Design by Cansaas Agency](https://contra.com/p/vk67o2ZR-budget-tracker-app-uiux-design)
