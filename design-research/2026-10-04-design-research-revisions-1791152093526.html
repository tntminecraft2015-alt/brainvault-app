Inset Shadows for Interactive Affordance

↻ Revising based on your rating: "Almost there"

Neumorphic inset shadows (light shadow at top-left, dark at bottom-right) layered subtly beneath Mission Control's hard JRPG borders create depth and signal interactivity on quest rows, event cards, and inputs without breaking retro style. Pair with strong text contrast to avoid neumorphism's accessibility trap. This makes clickable elements visually distinct from static labels at a glance.

Source: How to Master Neumorphism in UI Design (With Examples)

Device-Local Offline Simulation: Full 24h No-Connection Progress Banking

↻ Revising based on your rating: "Kind of"

Tideward simulates offline time entirely on-device so the full 24-hour window works with no connection at all—no server required. The simulator runs on your device with no account, so offline progression computes on a plane, in a tunnel, or with Wi-Fi off. On app close, it timestamps the save file along with active trade, gear, vitality, food, and buffs—then on return calculates elapsed wall-clock time (capped at 24 hours) and simulates what would have happened.

Source: Tideward — Offline Progression Comparison
