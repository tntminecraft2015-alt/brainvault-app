Entry-Level Micro-Rewards: Coins-for-Log (Dual Scroll Wheels)

↻ Revising based on your rating: "Just one more tweak" — "Make amount and category separate with a apple like scroll wheel the category"

Award small coin micro-rewards (+5 GIL) for each transaction logged or habit checked, with amount and category each selected via independent scroll wheels—one for coin value, one for the reward category (Training / Social / Gear / etc.). This keeps the immediate dopamine hit of logging tied directly to spending visibility, while letting players customize reward granularity and type in the same gesture.

Source: Guul Games: Gamification in Habit Tracking Apps
