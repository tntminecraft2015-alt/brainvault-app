Category Labels on Donut Slices (Enhanced)

↻ Revising based on your rating: "Just one more tweak" — "Make the labels stand out from the pie chart and make ur slideshow look better and more polished"

Place category names directly on the outer edge of each donut slice in high-contrast colored bands with white text, positioned tangent to the slice perimeter. This eliminates the separate legend entirely—users read spending instantly at a glance without cross-referencing. The enhanced label bands use dark backgrounds and bold typography to stand out sharply from the pie chart, making them scannable even on mobile.

Source: Budget Tracker App UI/UX Design by Cansaas Agency

Entry-Level Micro-Rewards: Coins-for-Log (Dual Scroll Wheels)

↻ Revising based on your rating: "Just one more tweak" — "Side by side and smaller boxes"

Award fractional GIL coins instantly when players log a transaction or check a habit, with reward amount and category selected via two compact side-by-side scroll wheels—no manual input, just spin-to-pick. The amount wheel spans 5.00 down to 0.01 GIL, letting you calibrate rewards by frequency: daily check = 0.10, weekly milestone = 0.50, big win = 2.50. This ties immediate dopamine (coin pop, wallet flash) directly to the act of logging, reinforcing visibility without requiring external motivation.

Source: Guul Games: Gamification in Habit Tracking Apps
