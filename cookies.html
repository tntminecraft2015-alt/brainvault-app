YouTube cookies for Cled

YouTube blocks the clip server. Cookies from a signed-in account let it download again.

  1. 1
    In Chrome, sign in to YouTube with a spare Google account (not your main one).
  2. 2
    Add Get cookies.txt LOCALLY to Chrome. You only do this once.
  3. 3
    Open youtube.com, click the extension, and click Copy (or Export to save a file).
  4. 4
    Come back here and press Ctrl+V (⌘V on a Mac), or drop the file in the box. It saves and checks itself.
Paste or drop your cookies here
Ctrl+V / ⌘V anywhere on this page works too

Only YouTube and Google cookies are kept, encrypted. Nothing is shown back on this page. Cled checks them once a day and tells you in the app when they need replacing.
