BrainClips Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

BrainClips is a personal tool run by its owner (part of the Mission Control app at brainvault-app.onrender.com). It turns stream videos into short clips and, when the owner chooses, posts them to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (Google) account: when the owner connects their channel, BrainClips receives permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel information such as its name (youtube.readonly). It uses these only to upload clips the owner approves and to show which channel is connected.
  • Public video sources: publicly available stream recordings the owner selects, to create clips.

What it stores

An encrypted sign-in token for the connected channel, the connected channel's name and ID, and the clips and captions it creates. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than making and posting the owner's clips.

Google user data

BrainClips' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect at any time from the Clips tab, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored token.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
